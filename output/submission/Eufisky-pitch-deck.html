05 Why it is original

Private help during a concerning call

  • Judge a call from caller ID alone
  • Ask an opaque model for a scam verdict
  • Record every family conversation
  • Take control away from the senior

Pause a concerning conversation, explain the evidence, and offer family help.

Explainable rules and private assistance. Consent controls need further hardening.
