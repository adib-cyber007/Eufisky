# Eufisky three-minute speaking notes

## 1. Eufisky

0:00-0:15. Eufisky is a voice agent that helps an older adult handle risky calls. It screens unknown callers and can pause a conversation for private support. This is a browser phone simulation built on AssemblyAI. Team Eufisky. Artwork: existing project favicon, app/web/static/favicon.svg, rasterized without design changes.

## 2. The risk inside the conversation

0:15-0:35. Adults aged 60 and older reported nearly 2.4 billion dollars in fraud losses in 2024. This figure covers all fraud, not just phone scams. Our illustrative scenario is an older adult facing an urgent Medicare impersonation call. Eufisky helps at the point where a stranger asks for private details. Source: FTC, Protecting Older Consumers 2024-2025, December 2025, https://www.ftc.gov/system/files/ftc_gov/pdf/P144400-OlderAdultsReportDec2025.pdf. Scenario is fictional, not a customer testimonial.

## 3. Guardian pauses the caller

0:35-0:55. This screenshot comes from the prerecorded Replay scenario in the deployed browser app. Risk reaches 76 after the caller creates urgency and requests Medicare and bank details. The state changes to Guardian. The caller stays on hold while the senior receives private help. Replay illustrates the policy and interface, not a live microphone session. ## 4. The call flow

0:55-1:15. Trusted contacts ring directly and bypass transcription and recording. Unknown callers meet Front Door. Connected unknown calls use separate caller and senior streaming sessions. Our deterministic policy scores speaker-specific evidence with decay. A private nudge starts at 40. Guardian holds the caller at 65, or earlier for defined sensitive disclosure/compliance rules. At 90 it recommends family support. Risk scores are heuristic thresholds, not probabilities. Source: docs/ARCHITECTURE.md, app/session/state_machine.py, app/rules/engine.py, current main.

## 5. AssemblyAI throughout the call

1:15-1:40. AssemblyAI powers voice screening and Guardian through its Voice Agent API, and separate Universal Streaming sessions for caller and senior. Keyterms include benign call vocabulary as well as names and scam phrases. The risk engine consumes finalized words. After microphone calls, batch transcription requests PII redaction for text and audio. The current LLM Gateway generates the incident summary with a deterministic template fallback. Typed calls use local numeric masking and have no microphone recording. Source: app/agent/voice_agent_backend.py, app/stt/assemblyai_stream.py, app/postcall/pipeline.py, current main. Per-leg labels do not depend on diarization.

## 6. Private help, reversible choices

1:40-2:00. The senior can resume the call, invite family, end the call or trust the caller. The caller remains held during a private family conference. Trusted calls bypass monitoring. Incident actions and outcomes come from persisted call events, so the summary reflects what actually happened. This screenshot is a preloaded sample report from Replay, not a report generated by a live call during this presentation. Source: app/phone/calls.py, app/postcall/pipeline.py, work/video/sample-report.png.

## 7. Voice reliability: checks and limits

2:00-2:20. We fixed fractional sample loss, PCM playback gaps, stale interrupted output and early hangup. The hosted browser integration check sent 130 valid 100-millisecond frames and received 5.4 seconds of provider audio without browser exceptions. The same synthetic recording now recognizes pharmacy delivery correctly. An unfamiliar name still joins two words. 89 Python tests and 4 dependency-free JavaScript audio checks pass. These are engineering checks, not a recognition accuracy or false-positive benchmark. Source: STATE.md voice reliability update, docs/HUMAN_TASKS.md deployment checks, commits d36f679, f59308a and fb63c23, work/voice-qa results.

## 8. The first buyer: an adult child

2:20-2:40. The initial buyer hypothesis is an adult child supporting a parent. A family subscription at 9 to 15 dollars per month is a hypothesis, not a live price or validated willingness to pay. The first pilot should measure recognition across accents and noise, false alarms on benign calls, response timing and user acceptance of private intervention. Real telephone integration and caller identity verification are needed before a carrier pilot. Telcos and senior-living operators are possible later distribution partners. Source: docs/BUSINESS.md, docs/TELEPHONY_ROADMAP.md, STATE.md. No paying customers or signed partnerships claimed.
