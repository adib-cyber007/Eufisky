05 Why it is original

Not another blocklist.

  • Judge a call from caller ID alone
  • Ask an opaque model for a scam verdict
  • Record every family conversation
  • Take control away from the senior

Speaker-aware, reversible intervention at the moment persuasion becomes dangerous.

The model talks. Deterministic policy decides. Margaret chooses.
